Privacy Policy · プライバシーポリシー

English日本語

Effective October 25, 2026

Bouncilio is a drawing-and-music toy for children. It is made to collect nothing.

We collect no data

  • The app has no accounts and never asks for a name, email address or any other personal information.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app contains no third-party SDKs and does not use the advertising identifier.
  • What you draw stays on your iPad and is never sent anywhere.
  • The app does not use the camera, microphone, photos, location or contacts.

What stays on your iPad

The app keeps a few settings on the device only, such as whether the unlock has been purchased (so it works offline). Deleting the app deletes them.

In-app purchase

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment information. The app only asks the App Store whether the unlock has been purchased. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases. Purchases are behind a question for grown-ups (a parental gate).

Children

Because the app collects no information at all, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
